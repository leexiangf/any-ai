lima-orca-vm-recipes

Disposable Lima VM workspaces — create, suspend, resume, and destroy with limactl.

Community VM recipe pack Lima VM Recipes (cemalkilic.lima-recipes). Each Orca workspace can run in its own Lima VM on your machine — no cloud account required. Needs limactl available on the host (typically macOS).
