kane-cli

Natural-language browser and mobile flow checks with real Chrome evidence.

Plugin kane-cli from the TestMu AI marketplace. Point the agent at a flow in plain language; Kane drives a real browser/mobile session and returns evidence instead of a blind “looks fine.”

Useful when you want Droid to open a path, click through critical steps, and leave artifacts you can re-check — not just assert text in a mock.
